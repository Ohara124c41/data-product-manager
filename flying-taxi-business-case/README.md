# Flyber MVP Launch Strategy

This project develops an evidence-based MVP launch strategy for Flyber, a proposed flying-taxi service in New York City. It uses comparable NYC taxi-trip data and user research to identify customer pain points, target conditions, viable launch opportunities, and product hypotheses before scaling a new mobility service.

## Product and market framing

Ground travel is valuable for commuting, airport transfers, appointments, tourism, and transit-gap trips, but riders face congestion, variable availability, uncertain arrival times, fare opacity, cancellations, and curb friction. Flyber’s early value proposition is time certainty: a scheduled, node-based service that can reduce travel-time variability for short, slow, time-sensitive trips while explicitly testing safety, noise, airspace, access, and equity risks.

## Analysis and MVP proposal

The completed proposal covers:

- Dataset scope, granularity, quality checks, descriptive statistics, and derived fare and duration-to-distance metrics.
- Passenger-volume, spatial-density, and temporal-demand analysis to identify the operating conditions and locations most suitable for an MVP.
- User-research segmentation, willingness-to-pay, adoption concerns, and product hypotheses.
- A staged MVP strategy that turns evidence into testable launch decisions rather than treating the flying-taxi concept as a guaranteed outcome.

## Reproducible source

The [`app/`](app/) folder contains the Python analysis, SQL queries, validation checks, and source notes used to support the proposal. Raw taxi and survey data remain local because the taxi export is large and the survey may contain respondent identifiers; they are intentionally excluded from this public portfolio repository.
